# Code of conduct

Regulatory Harvest contributors must help maintain a professional, inclusive, evidence-focused community.

## Expected conduct

- Treat contributors and users with respect, patience, and good faith.
- Critique ideas, evidence, code, and risks rather than people.
- Welcome differing legal, technical, geographic, and accessibility perspectives.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes without retaliation or ridicule.
- Protect confidential, personal, client, matter, employer, and security-sensitive information.
- Follow the clean-room and fixture-provenance rules even when a shortcut would be convenient.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, misuse of private information, and retaliation are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repositories, issues, reviews, discussions, community events, and other project spaces. Until a public moderation contact exists, report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner and do not include confidential details in a public issue. A public release must replace that temporary route with a monitored contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports should be handled promptly, impartially, confidentially to the extent practical, and without retaliation.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by widely used open-source community standards, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), without incorporating its text by reference as a separate license term.
